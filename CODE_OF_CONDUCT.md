# Community Conduct

JavaTavern welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, patient, and respectful in reviews.
- Critique code and product decisions, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing conversations or character cards without permission.
- Use content warnings where discussions include sensitive role-play material.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about safety, licensing, and project scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexual attention toward contributors, and publishing private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub reporting tools. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary.
